# Store page and art

Text for the Roblox page, and prompts for an AI image tool to make the icon,
thumbnails and pass icons. The art should only show things players find in
the game: two walkers facing off on a runway, the pink and aqua lanes, the
crowd with glowsticks, the crown and the podium.

## Store text

**Name:** Catwalk Clash

**Short pitch (for social posts and the first line):**
Dress up, strut the runway, and battle head-to-head for the crown!

**Description:**

> 👗 DRESS UP. STRUT. WIN THE CROWN. 👑
>
> Every round the lobby votes on a theme, from Red Carpet Gala to Space
> Explorer. You get 2 and a half minutes backstage to build your look from
> 150+ pieces in any colour, or try on anything from the Roblox catalog.
> Then it's SHOWTIME: walk the runway head-to-head with your entrance
> effect, strike your pose, and let the crowd and the judges decide who
> moves on. Win the bracket to take the crown and your statue in the plaza!
>
> ⭐ Head-to-head runway battles with live audience voting
> 🎨 150+ pieces, 36 colours and 8 finishes, plus the Roblox catalog
> ✨ 15 runway entrances and 14 poses
> 🏆 Style Score ranks, from Newcomer to Legend, and a world leaderboard
> 🎟️ Style Pass seasons with free rewards on every tier
> 📅 New themes every season and a double-reward spotlight theme every week
>
> Fair voting: you can't vote for yourself, and every outfit gets seen before
> votes count. Free outfits can win any theme.

**Genre:** Roleplay & Avatar Sim, subgenre Dress Up.

## Pass icons (512x512, Roblox crops them to a circle)

One prompt, changing the object in brackets: "A glossy 3D icon of [a gold
crown with pink gems / a glowing neon jacket and light-up cap / a pair of
petal fairy wings / a silver orbit crown with tiny planets / a paint palette
with a rainbow colour wheel / a big wardrobe with doors open / a gold VIP
ticket / a bag of sparkles / a jar of sparkles / a treasure chest overflowing
with sparkles], centered on a soft pink to purple gradient circle, sparkles
around it, no text."

Use them in this order: Style Pass, Neon Night, Fairy Garden, Star Couture,
Color Studio, Big Closet, VIP, then the three Sparkles packs. For Royal Gala,
Supermodel Poses and Showstopper Entrances use "a frost crown and crystal
slippers", "a blocky avatar striking a superstar pose" and "fireworks over a
runway".

## Before launch

- Check the image tool's terms allow commercial use, and record it in
  `docs/rights-register.md`.
- The thumbnails show a "VS" matchup, a crowd with glowsticks and a crown,
  all of which happen every round. If the game changes, make new art rather
  than leave a page that oversells it.
