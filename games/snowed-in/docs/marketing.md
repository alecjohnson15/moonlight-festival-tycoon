# Store art and text

The pass icons are done (`assets/marketing/passes/`, rendered from the game's
own models). The store icon and thumbnails still need making. For Halloween
Tycoon they were made with an AI image tool from prompts like these; do the
same, save them in `assets/marketing/` as `icon.png` (512x512) and
`thumbnail.png` (1920x1080), and check the tool's terms allow commercial use
(`docs/rights-register.md`).

## Icon (512x512)

> Roblox game icon, bright cartoon 3D style, a smiling blocky Roblox-style
> kid in a red bobble hat and scarf holding a flaming torch, standing in
> front of a huge glowing stone hearth fire in deep snow at night, a big
> fluffy white yeti with glowing blue eyes peeking over a snowy log wall
> behind them, aurora green and purple in the sky, falling snowflakes, warm
> orange firelight against cold blue snow, high contrast, simple readable
> shapes, centred subject, no text, no logos

## Thumbnail (1920x1080)

> Roblox game thumbnail, bright cartoon 3D style, wide shot of a cosy snowed-in
> mountain town at night, a giant glowing hearth fire in the town square with
> four blocky kids defending it: one swinging a torch, one firing a glowing
> ember slingshot, one building a log wall, one stirring a big cooking pot.
> Dozens of small round cartoon snow creatures with glowing blue eyes pouring
> in from a blizzard, a huge friendly-scary yeti stomping in the background,
> a steam train's headlight in the distance, aurora in the sky, warm orange
> vs cold blue, exciting and fun, not gory, no text, no logos

Second thumbnail, the "big number" style that worked for the tycoon:

> Roblox game thumbnail, bright cartoon 3D style, split image: left side a
> tiny campfire with one shivering blocky kid and the words space left blank,
> right side a huge blazing fortress of log walls, ember turrets and lanterns
> around a giant hearth with a team of kids cheering, snow monsters bouncing
> off the walls, aurora sky, high contrast, leave space at the top for text,
> no logos

Then add the text in any editor: **NIGHT 1** over the left, **NIGHT 10**
over the right, in a chunky rounded font.
