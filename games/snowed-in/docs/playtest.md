# Play-test checklist

For the first runs with real players (Studio's **Clients and Servers** test
with 2 to 4 players works too). Note the time each thing happens.

## First ten minutes

- [ ] Picked a role within 40 seconds (the role menu opens on its own)
- [ ] Knew what to do first without being told (the corner Goal card and the
      marker over the target)
- [ ] Chopped a pine and dropped the wood at the stockpile in under 60 s
- [ ] Fed the hearth before the first night
- [ ] Found the first frozen radio part on the first day or second day
- [ ] Understood being frozen and how to thaw a friend
- [ ] Built something with **B** without help

## A whole run

- [ ] Night 1 is survivable for one player who only gathers and feeds
- [ ] The hearth never felt hopeless or trivial (note the lowest flame)
- [ ] The yeti on night 5 was beaten and felt like an event
- [ ] All four radio parts fitted by about night 8
- [ ] Train arrived after night 10 (about minute 52); results screen shown
- [ ] Snowflakes, pass tiers and cabin decorations still there after
      rejoining

## Phones

- [ ] All buttons reachable with thumbs; nothing covers the hearth bar
- [ ] 30+ FPS on a mid-range phone during night 10 with 6+ players
- [ ] Build mode Place/Turn/Cancel buttons work

## Things to write down

Where people got stuck, what they said out loud, which role they picked
first, what they wanted to buy, and anything that looked broken.
