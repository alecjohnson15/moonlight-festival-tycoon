# Phantom Party: store page and art prompts

## Store text

**Name:** Phantom Party: Masquerade Mystery

**Short description (first lines people see):**

> One Phantom. One Guardian. Who's behind the mask? 🎭

**Description:**

> Welcome to the masked ball! Every round, one guest is secretly the Phantom,
> and only you know your own role.
>
> 🎭 PHANTOM: tag the guests before time runs out, and don't get caught.
> 🌙 GUARDIAN: find the Phantom and knock them out with the Moonbeam Blaster.
> 💖 MEDIC: revive a tagged guest, who saw who tagged them!
> 💡 SABOTEUR: cut the lights and help the Phantom.
> 🎉 GUEST: grab candy coins and clue pages to ring the Truth Bell, which
> shows where the Phantom is hiding.
>
> 🏰 Two huge maps: Hollowmere Manor and the Starlight Carnival, with secret
> passages, a mirror maze and a ferris wheel.
> 🎁 Collect over 60 masks, blades, blasters, knockout effects and trails. Open
> crates with coins you earn, trade with friends, and climb the Moon Pass.
> 💬 Whisper close by: in a round, only people near you can hear you.
>
> Cartoon knockouts, no blood. Have fun, and trust no one!

Swap the role emoji for plain bullets if the store shows them oddly.

## Tips

- Generate several and pick the clearest at phone size: shrink it to 150
  pixels wide and check you can still tell what's happening.
- Keep any text short and big; Roblox crops thumbnails slightly on some
  screens.
- Don't use real logos, other games' names or characters, or real people.
